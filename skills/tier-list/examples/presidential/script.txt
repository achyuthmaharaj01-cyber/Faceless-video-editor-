From steam powered limousines to a car that can swim, this is the Presidential Car Tier List.
## William Howard Taft | White Steamer | ~$4K | B | White Model M
William Howard Taft was the first president to buy official cars. In 1909 he turned the White House stables into a garage and filled it with a steam powered White Model M. B-tier.
## Lyndon B. Johnson | Amphicar | ~$3K | S | Amphicar 770
Lyndon B. Johnson kept an Amphicar at his Texas ranch. He would drive guests straight toward the lake, scream that the brakes had failed, and then float away. S-tier.
## John F. Kennedy | Lincoln Continental X-100 | ~$200K | A | Lincoln X-100
John F. Kennedy rode in a 1961 Lincoln Continental called the X-100. Ford leased it to the Secret Service for just 500 dollars a year. He also gave Americans a line they still quote today.
[CLIP: John F. Kennedy inaugural address 1961 | ask not what your country can do for you | 6]
After Dallas it was rebuilt and kept in service until 1977. Today it sits in the Henry Ford Museum. A-tier.
## Ronald Reagan | Jeep CJ-6 | ~$3K | C | 1962 Jeep CJ-6
Ronald Reagan drove a 1962 Jeep CJ-6 around his ranch in California. It had no doors, no roof, and he loved it more than any limousine. C-tier.
## Barack Obama | The Beast | ~$1.5M | D | Cadillac One
Barack Obama got the first version of the Beast in 2009. The doors are reportedly as heavy as an airplane door, and the president is not allowed to drive it. D-tier.
